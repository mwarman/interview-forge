# Mid-Level Java Engineer

## Opportunity

We are seeking a Mid-Level Java Engineer to join our core engineering team as we scale our cloud-native enterprise platforms to meet surging user demand. In this role, you will help tackle the complex technical challenges of modernization, shifting monolithic architectures toward highly resilient, event-driven microservices. You will build, optimize, and maintain the critical backend services that process millions of daily transactions, ensuring our systems remain secure, performant, and reliable as our business grows.

This is a highly hands-on backend engineering role that combines technical execution with emerging leadership. You will spend approximately 75% of your time writing clean production code, designing APIs, and configuring cloud infrastructure, and the remaining 25% mentoring junior developers and driving code quality through collaborative reviews. The ideal candidate is an experienced Java developer who has a strong grasp of modern cloud architectures, views technical mentoring as an investment in the team's velocity, and is eager to explore practical applications of AI integrations to optimize backend capabilities.

## Requirements

- 4+ years of professional backend software engineering experience using Java (Java 11 or higher)
- 3+ years of hands-on experience building web applications and microservices using the Spring Framework and Spring Boot
- 2+ years of experience designing, deploying, and maintaining production services on AWS (specifically EC2, ECS, Lambda, RDS, and S3)
- Proven experience designing and implementing secure, high-throughput RESTful APIs
- Solid understanding of relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) including schema design, query optimization, and connection pooling
- Experience practicing continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines using tools like GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins
- Familiarity or hands-on experience integrating AI/ML capabilities, large language models (LLMs), or vector databases into backend services (e.g., via LangChain4j, OpenAI APIs, AWS Bedrock) is highly desirable
- Demonstrated experience mentoring junior or associate engineers, conducting constructive code reviews, and pairing to elevate team capabilities

## Your Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain scalable microservices using Java and Spring Boot with an emphasis on code quality, security, and testability
- Architect and provision resilient AWS cloud infrastructure utilizing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles with Terraform or AWS CloudFormation
- Conduct rigorous, constructive code reviews and lead technical design sessions to maintain high engineering standards across the team
- Dedicate time to mentor junior and associate engineers through pair programming, architectural walk-throughs, and structured feedback
- Collaborate closely with product managers and cross-functional teams to translate complex business requirements into clear, executable technical designs
- Profile and optimize application performance, identifying and resolving database bottlenecks and latency issues in production environments

## You'll Love

- Shipping high-impact features directly to production using automated CI/CD pipelines that minimize friction and deployment overhead
- Balancing deep individual contributor focus time with high-signal, rewarding mentorship of enthusiastic junior developers
- Working within a mature engineering culture that treats technical debt as a first-class citizen and actively prioritizes architectural integrity
- Exploring and prototyping cutting-edge AI integrations to enhance core application workflows and automate backend decision-making data pathways

## How You Work

- You are a collaborative pragmatic—you advocate for architectural best practices and clean code, but you understand how to balance perfection with business delivery
- You don't rely on heavy project management frameworks; you are self-organized, proactive, and naturally break down ambiguous goals into actionable tasks
- You communicate with clarity and empathy, adjusting your technical depth whether you are explaining a system failure to stakeholders or guiding a junior engineer through a debugging session
- You possess a growth mindset, constantly learning new patterns and tools while remaining eager to pass that knowledge down to the rest of your team

## What Success Looks Like

- Within the first 90 days, you have successfully owned, designed, and deployed a core microservice or feature to production on AWS with minimal supervision
- The junior engineers under your guidance demonstrate measurable growth in their code quality, independence, and understanding of the Spring ecosystem
- Backend application metrics show quantifiable improvements in performance, code coverage, error reduction, and architectural maintainability under your watch
