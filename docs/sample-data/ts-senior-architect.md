# Senior Principal Application Architect & Full-Stack Engineer

## **Opportunity**

We are a premier technical consultancy specializing in designing, building, and deploying highly resilient, enterprise-scale digital platforms for Fortune 500 clients. Our engagements demand rapid, authoritative architectural leadership coupled with the ability to build production-ready systems from the ground up. We are seeking a Principal Application Architect with 25+ years of industry experience to lead technical teams, define future-state systems architecture, and bridge the gap between high-level business strategy and low-level code execution. This role matters because our clients trust us with their most complex, high-stakes modernization and digital transformation initiatives.

This is a heavily hands-on architecture and software engineering role. You will spend roughly 40% of your time authoring complex system design documents, architectural blueprints, and integration topologies, 40% of your time writing production-grade code alongside engineering teams to implement foundational components, and 20% leading engineering teams and advising client stakeholders during consulting engagements. The ideal candidate is a true industry veteran who pairs deep architectural wisdom with an active, modern coding practice, possessing the unique ability to command a boardroom of executives while simultaneously mentoring junior developers in the codebase.

## **Requirements**

- 25+ years of professional software development and systems engineering experience in enterprise-grade or high-growth environments
- 8+ years of experience acting as a Principal or Lead Application Architect, designing distributed systems, event-driven architectures, and hybrid-cloud integration patterns
- Expert-level, hands-on command of the full-stack JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem (Node.js, NestJS, Next.js, React, or modern equivalents)
- Deep, production-tested expertise with public cloud infrastructure, with a strong preference for Amazon Web Services (AWS) across services like EKS, ECS, Lambda, EventBridge, IAM, and VPC topologies
- Comprehensive understanding of data modeling, schema design, and performance tuning across both relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Aurora) and NoSQL datastores (DynamoDB, MongoDB, Redis)
- Proven experience architecting and building AI-integration patterns for business applications, including hands-on implementation of LLM orchestration frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex), vector databases (Pinecone, pgvector), and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines
- Demonstrated history of successfully leading cross-functional engineering teams during high-stakes client-facing consulting engagements or complex vendor evaluations
- Strong portfolio of creating comprehensive, clear system design documents (SDDs), technical RFCs, and interactive architectural diagrams

## **Your Responsibilities**

- Author and maintain definitive system design documents, sequence diagrams, and integration specifications for multi-million-user applications
- Write, review, and refactor mission-critical, full-stack TypeScript code, establishing core boilerplates, security patterns, and shared libraries for the development team
- Architect and construct highly scalable, fault-tolerant system integrations utilizing proven design patterns (e.g., CQRS, Event Sourcing, Circuit Breaker, Saga, and API Gateways)
- Lead technical teams on high-impact consulting engagements, acting as the primary technical point of contact for client leadership, enterprise architects, and executive sponsors
- Design and embed secure, cost-effective AI and machine learning integration workflows into core transactional business applications
- Define the data strategy and migration paths for shifting legacy monolithic data structures into optimized relational or distributed NoSQL environments on AWS
- Establish rigorous code quality standards, automated testing strategies, and secure CI/CD deployment infrastructure for client engineering teams

## **You'll Love**

- Blending deep, strategic system design with the creative gratification of daily, hands-on coding and implementation
- Solving highly unique, multifaceted technical puzzles for diverse client organizations, ensuring your work never becomes monotonous
- Acting as a trusted technical authority, influencing architectural direction and technical choices at the highest organizational levels
- Working in an environment that respects engineering craftsmanship, values technical depth, and rewards pragmatic, battle-tested solutions

## **How You Work**

- You are a pragmatic visionary—you design elegant, future-proof architectures but understand when to make intentional trade-offs to deliver business value
- You lead by example; you do not manage from an ivory tower, instead earning the respect of engineering teams by diving straight into the codebase alongside them
- You possess exceptional emotional intelligence and communication clarity, effortlessly translating complex cloud and AI concepts into plain language for non-technical stakeholders
- You approach architectural debates with a collaborative, data-driven mindset, focusing on root-cause analysis and objective requirements over personal technical dogma

## **What Success Looks Like**

- Within the first 90 days, you successfully assess a client's legacy infrastructure, deliver a comprehensive future-state system design document, and stand up a production-ready TypeScript/AWS boilerplate
- The engineering teams under your direction consistently deliver high-quality codebases that implement modern AI features (such as semantic search or predictive automation) with minimal architectural drift
- Your integration patterns eliminate systemic data silos across the client organization, measurably reducing system-to-system latency and API error rates by 40% or more
- Client stakeholders routinely re-engage our consultancy due to the technical clarity, engineering velocity, and strategic confidence you establish during your engagements
